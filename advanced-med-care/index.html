About us

Clinical leadership rooted in emergency care and community service.

Advanced Med Care is led by experienced medical and emergency-care professionals who bring together clinical expertise, operational leadership, training capability and a strong commitment to serving Kimberley and the wider Northern Cape.

Medical Leadership

Dr Neil Cupido

Director and Chief Medical Officer

Dr Neil Cupido is a dedicated and compassionate General Practitioner, renowned for his commitment to healthcare excellence in the Northern Cape. He earned his medical degree from the University of Stellenbosch, laying a strong academic foundation for a career centred on service and clinical excellence.

Dr Cupido completed his internship at Groote Schuur Hospital, one of South Africa’s leading tertiary institutions, where he gained extensive experience across various fields of medicine. He went on to provide critical community service at Kimberley Hospital, making a meaningful impact in underserved areas.

He currently works in the Emergency Unit at Lenmed Royal Hospital and Heart Centre, where he is known for quick decision-making, calm under pressure and life-saving expertise.

Beyond the hospital environment, Dr Cupido is a private practitioner who owns and manages several medical practices in and around central Kimberley. Through these practices — and through Advanced Med Care — he continues to serve the community with passion, dedication and an unwavering commitment to quality care.

Emergency Medical Services

André Keyser

Advanced Life Support Paramedic • Advanced Med Care — Kimberley, Northern Cape

André Keyser is a highly skilled Advanced Life Support (ALS) Paramedic who qualified from Lebone College of Emergency Care in 2005. With a career spanning more than two decades, André brings extensive clinical expertise, leadership and operational insight to emergency medical services.

He began his career in Emergency Services in 1999 and, through dedication and continuous professional development, progressed into Advanced Medical Emergency Care.

In addition to his ALS qualification, André is also a certified Firefighter, Training Instructor and Advanced Driving Instructor, making him a multifaceted professional in pre-hospital care, rescue and emergency-response training.

André’s career has extended beyond South Africa, with experience across Ethiopia, Kenya, Zambia and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. This international exposure has strengthened his expertise in remote-site medical support, cross-border EMS operations and high-pressure environments.
